Population is not electorate, registered electorate is not turnout, and online activity is not a representative sample. The product must keep those denominators separate.

## Geography, urbanicity, language, and socioeconomic context

National views use the poll-industry macro-regions NCR, Balance Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao only as a comparison layer. Operational collection retains the underlying PSA/COMELEC region whenever available. Urban/rural, language, income, education, occupation, religion, ethnicity, and political affiliation must not be guessed from a person, account, location, or content.

Filipino and English are the national working-language baseline. Cebuano/Bisaya, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Bikol and other languages are source-coverage requirements where relevant, not identity proxies. Translation must retain the original excerpt and record the translation provider and confidence.

Socioeconomic indicators may be used only at published aggregate geography and with the source period attached. The POC does not create household wealth estimates or merge commercial identity data with political activity.

## Eight aggregate demo cohorts

The cohorts are intentionally broad, non-exclusive research lenses. They are backed by the COMELEC age bands, PSA population and connectivity data, and the four-region reporting convention in the Pulse Asia July 2026 survey. No cohort reaction is a poll result.

| Synthetic archetype | Aggregate definition | Evidence basis | Design purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metro first-time and young voters | NCR, 18–30, mixed Filipino/English, urban | COMELEC age band + PSA NCR population + connectivity | Check concise digital-message clarity |
| Balance Luzon working-age commuters | Balance Luzon, 31–59, urban/peri-urban | COMELEC age band + regional geography | Check cost, service, and delivery relevance |
| Balance Luzon rural households | Balance Luzon, adult, rural | PSA geography/urbanicity tables when loaded | Check offline reach and local specificity |
| Visayas connected youth | Visayas, 18–30, urban/peri-urban, Filipino/Cebuano mix | COMELEC age band + PSA connectivity | Check language and short-video comprehension |
| Visayas working families | Visayas, 31–59, mixed urbanicity | COMELEC age band | Check credibility and household-economy framing |
| Mindanao connected youth | Mindanao, 18–30, urban/peri-urban, Filipino/Cebuano mix | COMELEC age band + PSA connectivity | Check regional relevance without identity inference |
| Mindanao working communities | Mindanao, 31–59, mixed urbanicity | COMELEC age band | Check local-service and peace/order specificity |
| National older news followers | Philippines, 60+, mixed urbanicity, Filipino/English | COMELEC senior age band | Check broadcast/news readability and recall |

Each card must name its aggregate basis, use at least three parallel synthetic runs, and show consensus plus variance across the six qualitative criteria. It must not recommend the “best target segment,” estimate vote share, or claim individual response.

## Sensitive-context rule

Ethnicity, religion, health, political affiliation, family relationships, and inferred economic status are sensitive context. They may appear only in cited, aggregate research needed to interpret public conditions. They are prohibited as individual targeting fields, join keys, or inferred attributes.
